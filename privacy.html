Vidday Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how the Vidday mobile app ("the App"), developed by Roman Shtykalo ("I", "me"), handles your information. I am the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Data stored only on your device

Everything you enter in the App — items, people's names, dates, notes, photos and reminders — is stored locally on your device. It is never sent to me or to any server I operate. If you pick a contact, only the display name is copied into the App. Your data may be included in your device's own backup (Google or iCloud), which is governed by Google's or Apple's privacy policies.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, if you allow it)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostics

This data is used to show, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown; you can choose non-personalized ads. On iOS, tracking happens only if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

PRO subscribers do not see ads, and the ads SDK does not request ads for them.

3. Purchases (RevenueCat, Google Play, App Store)

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. I never receive your payment details. RevenueCat, Inc. manages subscription status on my behalf and processes an anonymous app user ID, purchase history, device type and IP address. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Contract — processing purchases and providing PRO features.
  • Consent — personalized advertising and tracking. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interest — showing non-personalized ads, fraud prevention and keeping the App secure.

5. International transfers

Google and RevenueCat may process data outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States, under Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

6. Retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Third-party providers keep data according to their own policies.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Because I hold no personal data about you on my servers, most requests are best handled by deleting the App's data or uninstalling it, or by contacting Google or RevenueCat directly. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To limit ad personalization: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads; on iOS, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU), and I do not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Changes

I may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
